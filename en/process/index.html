How a drone mapping project works

Every DroneAtlas mapping project follows the same seven steps, from the question "what do you need to decide?" to the files in your folder. Getting the definition right in step one is what makes the deliverable fit for use.

1. Define purpose, area and accuracy

What will the deliverable be used for: design, billing, marketing or submission? We define the area boundary, deliverables, formats, coordinate system, accuracy and check method, and whether a licensed surveyor's signature is needed. All of it goes into the proposal.

2. Airspace and approvals

We check airspace restrictions at the site and coordinate with the relevant bodies where needed. Flights are flown by a CAAI-licensed pilot [TO FILL: licence number] with a registered drone [TO FILL: registration number] and third-party insurance [TO FILL]. In built-up areas or near people and infrastructure, we get written consent from the site owner and apply for a special authorisation when the standard rules do not allow the flight.

3. Flight planning

We set flight height, image overlap, camera angles and time of day to match the resolution and deliverable required. For repeat rounds, the same plan is kept.

4. Ground control and check points

Where survey accuracy is needed, we place and survey ground control points to anchor the model, plus separate check points to verify accuracy at the end. Equipment: [TO FILL: drone model, RTK/PPK system and GNSS receiver].

5. On-site flight

We coordinate with the person in charge on site in advance, check weather and ground conditions, and fly the plan. After the flight we confirm coverage on site.

6. Processing and quality control

Images are processed in photogrammetry software WebODM (OpenDroneMap). Deliverables are produced and checked against the check points, and the result is documented in a quality-control report.

7. Delivery

We deliver files in the agreed formats with the quality report and a viewing link where needed. If you have questions about the deliverable, we are available to explain and adjust.

Process FAQ

How long from order to delivery?

It depends on area, deliverables and the approvals needed. The schedule is set in the proposal [TO FILL: typical turnaround].

What if the weather is not suitable?

Strong wind, rain or fog delays the flight. In that case we set a nearby alternative date.

What do you need from us?

The site location and boundary, the purpose of the mapping, an on-site contact and, if available, design files or a previous survey.
