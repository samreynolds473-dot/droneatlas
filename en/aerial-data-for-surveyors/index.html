Drone capture and processed data for surveyors

DroneAtlas works with licensed surveyors as a supplier of aerial capture and processed data. We plan and fly the job and, if you ask, process the data. You remain the professional who surveys, checks and signs. DroneAtlas is not a licensed surveyor and does not present its deliverables as official survey documents.

What you can order

OptionWhat you receive
Flight onlyRaw geotagged images, flight log and flight plan
Flight + processingOrthophoto (GeoTIFF/ECW), point cloud (LAS/LAZ) and DSM on the Israeli Grid (EPSG:2039)
With ground controlTied to the control points you mark and survey, with an RMS report against check points
Repeat roundsThe same flight plan every visit, for consistent comparison

How it works

  1. You send the polygon, required GSD, control method and deliverables.
  2. We check airspace, coordinate and fly.
  3. We deliver the data in a fixed folder structure, with metadata stating the method (RTK, ground control or both) and intended use.
  4. You take it from there: survey, field infill, drafting and signature.

Why work with us

  • A flight plan built around your work: overlap, height and angles to suit the product you produce.
  • Clean handoff: consistent file names, coordinate system and metadata on every job.

FAQ for surveyors

Are the deliverables suitable for a map under the Survey Regulations?

That depends on the purpose, method and camera. Photogrammetric products under the regulations require an approved camera system with a valid calibration certificate. We state the equipment up front, and you decide whether it suits your product.

Who signs the map?

You do. DroneAtlas supplies data only and does not sign survey documents.

Which coordinate system do I get?

The Israeli Grid (EPSG:2039) by default, or another system on request.
