Industrial drone photography and video

Industrial drone photography is stills and video of plants, infrastructure and construction sites, from angles you cannot reach from the ground.

  • JPG
  • MP4 4K
  • 16:9 and 9:16
  • All of Israel

DroneAtlas shoots high-resolution stills and 4K video, with editing included, so the material is ready for records, presentations and marketing. This is not a measurable map. When you need a map, use geodetic mapping. When you need heat or the condition of an envelope, use visual and thermal inspection.

01

Who it is for

  • Construction sites round-by-round records for owners, developers and lenders. See also progress mapping.
  • Plants and facilities presentations, reports and a condition record.
  • Infrastructure roads, lines and energy facilities, for records and presentation. See infrastructure and municipalities.
  • Quarries a picture of the whole site, without walking into the plant area.

02

How it works

  1. Brief what the film or stills must show, and for whom.
  2. Route heights and angles, vertical and oblique, without passing over workers and operating plant.
  3. Capture stills, and 4K video with planned moves, in landscape (16:9) and vertical (9:16).
  4. Repeat the same viewpoints when you want to show change over time.
  5. Edit shot selection, colour, transitions and licensed music [TO FILL: music source and licensing].

04

Accuracy and quality control

This page is records and communication, not a coordinate survey. There is no RMS report. If the images also need to sit on the Israeli Grid, order mapping alongside and say so in the proposal.

Flying over people, vehicles or infrastructure needs the site owner's written consent and sometimes a special CAAI authorisation. We check this in advance.

05

When to use it, and when to choose something else

  • A map, a volume or contours that is geodetic mapping or volumes, not a film.
  • A finding on an envelope, moisture or a hot spot visual and thermal inspection.
  • A signed map does not come from a photograph. DroneAtlas is not a licensed surveyor. The deliverables are for design, control and quantities. A statutory survey plan, such as a permit map, a registration plan or a boundary determination, is signed by a partner licensed surveyor. Photogrammetry is not used to reconstruct registered boundaries. If a map shows boundaries and is not for registration, the surveyor marks it "not for registration purposes".

06

Limits and approvals

The service covers all of Israel, subject to flight approvals and airspace limits at the site. Commercial operation of a small drone requires a small-UAS pilot licence from the Civil Aviation Authority [TO FILL: licence number] and a registered aircraft [TO FILL: registration numbers]. We carry third-party insurance [TO FILL: policy details]. In a built-up area, or when flying over people, vehicles or infrastructure, we need the site owner's written consent and sometimes a special authorisation. We coordinate in advance with the person in charge on site.

Strong wind, rain or fog delays the flight, and we set a nearby alternative date. Capture is planned to stay on the commissioned area, and we do not publish material that identifies people or third-party property. Turnaround depends on area, deliverables and approvals, and is set in the proposal [TO FILL: typical turnaround].

07

What to prepare

  • The site location, a map pin or a boundary file (KML, KMZ, DWG, DXF, SHP or GeoJSON).
  • Approximate size, the purpose, the deliverables you want and a preferred date.
  • An on-site contact, and access on the flight day.
  • If you have them: a georeferenced design file (DWG or SHP) or a previous survey.
  • A shot list: what must be seen, and what must not be published.

08

FAQ

How long until I get the edited video?

It depends on the shoot and the edit, and is set in the proposal [TO FILL: typical turnaround].

Who owns the material?

Usage terms are set out in the proposal [TO FILL: copyright and licensing policy].

Can you film inside an active site?

Yes, coordinated with the site manager and on a route that avoids workers and operating plant.

Does the photography include thermal?

No. Thermal imaging is a separate service on the visual and thermal inspection page. The two can be scheduled for the same visit if the proposal says so.
