Drone mapping and survey FAQ

Short, direct answers to the questions we hear most. Each answer links to the service page with more detail.

Concepts

What is an orthophoto?

An orthophoto is an aerial image that has been geometrically corrected and georeferenced, so every point sits in its true position. You can measure distances and areas on it and overlay plans. More on orthophotos

What is photogrammetry?

Photogrammetry measures 3D geometry from images. From overlapping photos of the same area, the position and height of every point are computed, producing maps, elevation models and 3D models. More on drone surveys

What is a digital twin?

A digital twin is a digital copy of a physical asset, such as a site, building or structure, that can be measured and updated over time. In drone mapping it starts with an accurate 3D model of existing conditions. More on 3D models

What is the difference between DSM and DTM?

A DSM models the surface including buildings, vegetation and stockpiles. A DTM models the bare ground only.

Accuracy and validity

What is the difference between resolution and accuracy?

Resolution is the ground area one pixel represents. Accuracy is how close a map position is to the true position. A resolution of 1 cm per pixel does not mean 1 cm accuracy, so accuracy and its check method are defined separately.

Does a drone survey replace a licensed surveyor?

For design, construction control and quantities, the drone deliverable stands on its own. A building permit map, a registration plan or a boundary survey must be signed by a licensed surveyor, and at DroneAtlas these are signed by a partner licensed surveyor. If a map shows boundaries and is not for registration, the surveyor marks it "not for registration purposes".

Photogrammetry or LiDAR?

Photogrammetry suits open ground, construction sites and stockpiles, and also produces a colour orthophoto. LiDAR is better when you need a ground model beneath dense vegetation.

Approvals and safety

What approvals does a mapping flight need?

Commercial operation of a small drone in Israel requires a small-UAS pilot licence from the Civil Aviation Authority and a registered aircraft. We also carry third-party insurance [TO FILL: policy details]. Some areas also need airspace coordination, and flying over people or beyond visual line of sight may need special authorisation.

Do you fly anywhere in Israel?

We serve all of Israel. For every site we check airspace restrictions and the approvals needed in advance.

What about privacy?

We plan capture to focus on the commissioned area, and we do not publish material that identifies people or third-party property.

Ordering and cost

How much does drone mapping cost?

There is no fixed price list; every proposal is quoted by scope: area, required accuracy, deliverables, ground control, whether a surveyor's signature is needed, and the number of rounds. Send us the project details and we will reply with a proposal.

Which files do I receive?

Depending on the order: orthophoto (GeoTIFF, ECW), elevation models (GeoTIFF), contours (DWG, DXF, SHP), point cloud (LAS, LAZ), textured 3D model (OBJ, 3D Tiles), volume report (PDF, XLSX), and photos and 4K video (JPG, MP4). Processing is done in WebODM (OpenDroneMap).

How long until delivery?

What do you need from me for a quote?

The site location (a map pin or boundary file), approximate size, the purpose, the deliverables you want and a preferred date.
