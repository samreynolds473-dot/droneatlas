Surveyors & planners

Drones for surveyors and planners

An aerial base layer and LiDAR point clouds that drop straight into CAD and GIS.

Services

  • 01

    Drone data for surveyors

    You survey and sign. We bring the aerial layer.

    A licensed surveyor receives an aerial base layer from us: an orthophoto, elevation model and point cloud, tied to their own control points. The survey and the signature stay with the surveyor, and the aerial data saves field time on large sites.

    What you receive

    • Orthophoto and elevation models
    • Point cloud
    • Quality-control report
    View service

  • 02

    Topographic survey

    Complements the ground survey; does not replace it.

    On a large or hard-to-reach site, measuring every point on the ground takes days. A drone topographic survey covers the whole area as a continuous surface, and you complete in the field whatever needs point accuracy or a signature. Deliverables arrive in the formats and coordinate system your office already uses.

    What you receive

    • DTM and DSM as a continuous surface
    • Contours at the interval you choose
    • Files ready for AutoCAD and Civil 3D
    • Orthophoto as a drawing background
    View service

  • 03

    LiDAR mapping

    A classified point cloud, for ground the camera cannot see.

    Where the ground is covered by vegetation, photogrammetry gives the height of the canopy, not the ground. A LiDAR scan produces a dense point cloud in which ground is separated from vegetation and buildings, so a reliable DTM, sections and contours can be derived even in scrub, in a wadi or along a route.

    What you receive

    • Classified point cloud, LAS or LAZ
    • DTM from open and covered ground
    • Sections and contours
    • Coordinate system on request
    View service

Drone Atlas is not a licensed surveyor. The deliverables are for design, control and quantities. A statutory survey plan, such as a permit map or a boundary determination, is signed by a partner licensed surveyor.
