About DroneAtlas

DroneAtlas is a drone mapping and survey company serving all of Israel. We turn aerial imagery into maps, models and numbers you can make decisions on: to design, bill, track and present.

Why "Atlas"

An atlas is a collection of maps. We want every project we map to have a clear, accurate map that everyone involved can understand and use.

How we work

  • Define before we fly. Purpose, accuracy, formats and schedule are written in the proposal.
  • Say what the deliverable is for, and what it is not for. DroneAtlas is not a licensed surveyor. Statutory survey plans are signed by a partner licensed surveyor, and we say so up front.
  • Check our own work. Every survey deliverable is checked against check points and comes with a report.
  • Fly by the rules. Pilot licence, aircraft registration, airspace checks and special authorisations where needed.
