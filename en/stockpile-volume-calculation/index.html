Stockpile volume and earthwork calculation by drone

Drone stockpile and earthwork volume calculation uses an accurate elevation model of the site, computed from drone imagery. From it we calculate the volume of each pile, cut and fill against a reference surface, and the change between two dates. DroneAtlas provides volume surveys for contractors, quarries, plants and construction sites across Israel.

Monthly volume contracts for quarries

For a quarry, earthworks site or materials yard, the real value is consistency. On the first visit we build a baseline model and define the pile boundaries and base surfaces. From month two, every survey uses the same method, so month-to-month change is reliable. It suits inventory reconciliation, extraction tracking and checking against invoices. Each proposal is quoted by site size and survey frequency.

How drone volumes are calculated

  1. The drone photographs the site on a planned route with high overlap.
  2. A point cloud and surface model (DSM) are computed from the images.
  3. For each pile or zone we define a base surface: the ground beneath the pile, a design surface, or a previous survey.
  4. Volume is the difference between the surface and the base surface.
  5. We deliver a report with the volume per pile and zone, a map of the boundaries, and the files.

What you receive

  • A volume report (PDF and XLSX) per pile and zone, stating the calculation method.
  • Cut and fill against design or the previous round.
  • An orthophoto of the site with pile boundaries. See orthophoto.
  • The elevation model and point cloud (GeoTIFF, LAS) for independent checking.
  • Periodic tracking: the same method and route each round, so comparisons stay consistent.

Why measuring from the air suits stockpiles and earthworks

  • Safety: nobody climbs piles or walks into heavy-plant areas.
  • Full coverage: the whole pile surface is measured, not a few points around the edge.
  • Consistency: the survey can be repeated exactly, so month-to-month comparisons hold up.
  • Transparency: the report, map and model let every party see how the number was reached.

When volumes are used for billing

When a volume figure is used for an interim payment, a claim or a submission to an authority, agree the calculation method and base surface with all parties up front. Where a signed quantity is required, it is signed by a partner licensed surveyor. Without that signature, the report is a professional volume estimate with the method and uncertainty stated, not a certified quantity.

Who it is for

Earthworks and infrastructure contractors, construction sites, quarries, concrete and asphalt plants, waste and recycling sites, and ports and bulk-material yards. See also construction progress monitoring and topographic survey.

Volume calculation FAQ

How accurate is a drone stockpile survey?

It depends on ground control, positioning, flight height and how the base surface is defined. We set the required accuracy and check method in the proposal and state them in the report.

What is a base surface and why does it matter?

It is the surface the volume is measured from, for example the ground under a pile. A different base surface gives a different number, so we define it up front and document it in the report.

How often should we measure?

It depends on the pace of work and billing needs. Some sites measure monthly and others more often. We will match the frequency to your site.

Can you measure stockpiles inside a closed building?

Standard photogrammetry is for open-air sites. A closed warehouse needs a different approach, which we assess case by case.
