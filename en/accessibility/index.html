Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people with disabilities.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Accessibility features

The website was built to meet the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

  • Full keyboard navigation, with a “skip to content” link at the top of every page.
  • A logical heading structure and landmarks that describe each part of the page.
  • Text alternatives for images that carry information.
  • An accessibility menu: larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, a readable font and stopping animations and videos.
  • Layouts that work on phones and desktops and with browser zoom.

If something is not accessible

Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. If you run into a problem, please tell us through the contact page: which page, what you were trying to do, and which browser or assistive technology you used. We will deal with it promptly.

Not sure what your project needs?

Send the site location, rough area, what the work is for and your preferred date. We will recommend the deliverables and send a clear proposal.
