Drone mapping in two sentences

Drone mapping means photographing a site from the air on a planned route, in hundreds of overlapping images that photogrammetry software turns into a measurable map and model. You get a complete, up-to-date picture of the site on which you can measure distances, areas, heights and volumes, without sampling it point by point on foot.

Orthophoto · DTM · Point cloud · 3D

Our services

For contractors and developers: construction progress mapping sets. The same flight route on every visit, and each round an orthophoto of the site or a 3D model, a design check and earthwork quantities. Construction progress mapping

For quarries and earthworks: monthly volume surveys. The same method and base surface every month, and a volume report per pile and zone. Stockpile and earthwork volumes

Our five services:

  • RTK geodetic mapping: orthophoto, DSM and DTM, contours, point cloud and volume measurements on the Israeli Grid.
  • Ground control points (GCP): layout, marking and GNSS measurement, with check points and an error report.
  • Terrain-follow mapping: a constant height above ground over slopes and quarries.
  • 3D modelling and point clouds: textured models of buildings or sites you can rotate and measure.
  • Industrial drone photography: stills and 4K video with professional editing.

And for surveyors: drone data for surveyors. We fly and process; the surveyor surveys and signs.

Full process

Why drone mapping, not only ground survey?

  • Full coverage instead of samplingYou get the entire surface, not just the points that were measured.
  • Faster on large, open sitesField time is planned up front according to site size.
  • SaferStockpiles, slopes and active areas are measured from the air, without anyone walking on them.
  • RepeatableThe same flight plan each round makes comparisons between dates clear.
  • A picture everyone understandsOrthophotos and 3D models make it easier for developers, designers, contractors and authorities to discuss the same site.

DroneAtlas is not a surveying firm. Our mapping deliverables are for design, control and quantities. When a statutory survey plan is needed, such as a permit map, it is signed by a partner licensed surveyor. We say so in the proposal, so there are no surprises at the approvals stage.

Questions

FAQ

What is an orthophoto?

An orthophoto is an aerial image that has been geometrically corrected, so every point sits in its true map position. That is why you can measure distances and areas on it and overlay plans.

Does a drone survey replace a licensed surveyor?

Not always. For design, construction control and quantities, the drone deliverable stands on its own. A statutory survey plan, such as a permit map, is signed by a partner licensed surveyor. DroneAtlas itself is not a licensed surveyor.

Where do you work?

Across Israel, subject to flight approvals and airspace restrictions at each site.

How much does it cost?

There is no fixed price list. Each proposal is quoted by scope: area, required accuracy, deliverables, ground control and number of rounds. Send us the details and we will reply with a proposal.
