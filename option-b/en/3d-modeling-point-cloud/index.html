Drone 3D modelling and point clouds

A drone 3D model is an accurate, full-colour reconstruction of a site, building or facade, built from hundreds of images taken from different angles. You can rotate it, measure distances, heights and areas on it, and bring it into your design work. DroneAtlas produces point clouds, meshes and existing-condition digital twins for projects across Israel.

What is a digital twin?

A digital twin is a digital copy of a physical asset that is updated over time. In mapping, the first step is an accurate model of existing conditions. Capturing it again in rounds lets you compare the models and track change.

What you receive

  • A point cloud as LAS or LAZ, for design and survey software.
  • A textured mesh as OBJ or FBX, plus 3D Tiles for web viewing.
  • A browser viewing link that also works on a phone, for sharing with clients and partners.
  • Measurements from the model: heights, distances, facade and roof areas.
  • Where needed: an orthophoto and elevation model from the same flight.

Common uses

  • Design and renovation: existing conditions of a building and facade, including details that are hard to measure from the ground.
  • Urban renewal: recording existing buildings and their surroundings.
  • Supervision and records: site condition before and after a phase. See construction progress monitoring.
  • Presenting the project: visualising it on the real site for developers and committees.
  • Quantity control: a point cloud as the base for volume calculation.

What affects model quality

  • Camera angles: facades also need oblique images, not just vertical ones.
  • Distance to the subject: closer capture gives sharper detail.
  • Light and weather: even light gives better texture.
  • Difficult surfaces: glass, water and shiny surfaces are hard to reconstruct, so we plan for them.

3D model FAQ

What is the difference between a point cloud and a mesh?

A point cloud is millions of points with position and colour, and it is the base for measurement and design. A mesh is a continuous textured surface that is easier to view and present.

Can I open the model without special software?

Yes. You can get a browser viewing link as well as files for professional software.

Is a drone 3D model suitable for BIM?

A drone point cloud can serve as the base for as-built BIM modelling. Its level of detail depends on capture planning, so we define it up front.
