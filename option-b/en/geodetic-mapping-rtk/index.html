Geodetic area mapping with RTK drones

Geodetic drone mapping means a planned drone flight with RTK positioning, followed by photogrammetric processing into deliverables georeferenced to the Israeli Grid: an orthophoto, elevation models, contours and a point cloud. DroneAtlas maps areas of any size across Israel, from a single plot to large sites. The deliverables are ready for AutoCAD, Civil 3D, QGIS and ArcGIS.

Processing is done in WebODM (OpenDroneMap), open-source photogrammetry software. The default coordinate system is the Israeli Grid (EPSG:2039).

RTK, ground control and accuracy

RTK gives every image a precise position at capture time. Ground control points (GCPs) tie the model to the ground, and separate check points test it. On hilly or steep ground

On slopes, wadis and quarry faces, flying at a fixed height above the take-off point gives uneven resolution. In those cases we fly terrain-follow mapping.

Common uses

  • Existing conditions for design: a base for plans and engineering design, with a DTM and contours.
  • Construction sites: repeat mapping rounds. See construction progress mapping.
  • Quarries and earthworks: volumes and cut and fill. See volume surveys.
  • Surveyors: an aerial base layer for your work. See drone data for surveyors.

What the mapping is and is not

The deliverables are for design, control and quantities. DroneAtlas is not a licensed surveyor: statutory survey plans, such as permit maps, are signed by a partner licensed surveyor. Photogrammetry is not used to reconstruct registered boundaries.

Geodetic mapping FAQ

What is an orthophoto?

An orthophoto is an aerial image that has been geometrically corrected and georeferenced, so every point sits in its true position. You can measure on it like a map.

What is the difference between DSM and DTM?

A DSM includes everything on the surface: buildings, trees and stockpiles. A DTM is the bare ground only, and is the base for contours and design.

Which file formats do I get?

Orthophotos and elevation models as GeoTIFF, contours as DXF/DWG, SHP or GeoPackage, and point clouds as LAS or LAZ. If you need another format, we agree it up front.

How large an area can you map?

From a single plot to large open areas. Area size sets the number of flights and processing time, and goes into the proposal.

Can the mapping be submitted to a planning committee?

As a background layer, yes. A permit map is signed by a partner licensed surveyor.
