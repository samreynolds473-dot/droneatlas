Aerial mapping, survey and documentation services

Construction progress mapping Stockpile and earthwork volumes Drone data for surveyors RTK geodetic mapping Ground control points (GCP) Terrain-follow mapping 3D modelling and point clouds Industrial drone photography

DroneAtlas offers five services. Each ends in a defined deliverable, file format and check method agreed up front. We build our solutions for contractors and quarries from these services.

The five services

ServiceWhat you receive
RTK geodetic mappingGeoTIFF orthophoto, DSM and DTM, contours, LAS/LAZ point cloud, area and volume measurements
Ground control points (GCP)Layout, marking and GNSS measurement, check points, point file and error report
Terrain-follow mappingMapping on hilly and steep ground at even resolution
3D modelling and point cloudsTextured OBJ and 3D Tiles model, point cloud, on-model measurements
Industrial drone photographyHigh-resolution stills and edited 4K video

Solutions by client

  • Construction progress mapping for contractors and developers: geodetic mapping and a 3D model each round, on the same route.
  • Monthly volume surveys for quarries and earthworks: a DSM and volume report every month.
  • Drone data for surveyors: flight, ground control and processing; the surveyor signs.

What every service includes

  • Agreed scope: purpose, area, accuracy, formats and delivery date, written in the proposal.
  • Legal, coordinated flights: a CAAI-licensed pilot [TO FILL: licence number], a registered aircraft [TO FILL: registration number], third-party insurance [TO FILL], and airspace checks and authorisations where needed.
  • Processing and QC: processed in WebODM (OpenDroneMap) and checked against check points whenever survey accuracy is required.
  • Work-ready files: on the Israeli Grid (EPSG:2039), in the formats your team already uses.
  • Statutory survey plans are signed by a partner licensed surveyor where the purpose requires it. DroneAtlas itself is not a licensed surveyor.
  • Quoted by scope: there is no fixed price list.
