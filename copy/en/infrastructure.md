Drone mapping for infrastructure companies and local authorities: roads, utility lines, pipelines, and compounds the authority is planning or maintaining. All of it is subject to flight approvals and airspace limits.

The useful product is existing conditions: an orthophoto, an elevation model or a photographic record, which a design can sit on. There are no authority names, company names or sample projects here.

## Who it is for

- **A roads or water company** a long corridor, sometimes on a slope, that needs even coverage.
- **A local authority** a base for planning and for an internal discussion. A submission that needs a signed map goes through a partner licensed surveyor.
- **Energy sites and lines** a condition record, and separately a visual or thermal inspection when that is the question.
- **A design team** files for CAD and GIS on the Israeli Grid.

## How it works

1. **Define the corridor or the compound** and what must come out: a map, heights, photography, or an inspection.
2. **Check the airspace early** lines, roads and built-up land often sit under restrictions. Without approval we do not fly.
3. **Plan the flight** for a hilly corridor, [terrain-follow mapping](/en/terrain-follow-mapping/).
4. **Process in WebODM** onto the Israeli Grid, when a measurable map is ordered.
5. **Deliver** files and a report. For a bridge, tower, tank or solar array, see [visual and thermal inspection](/en/drone-ndt-inspection/), which can be scheduled for the same area if the proposal includes both.

"As built" here means a record of existing conditions after construction, not a signed record plan. A signed plan is the partner licensed surveyor's, when one is required.

## Accuracy and quality control

RTK gives every image a position at capture. [Ground control points](/en/ground-control-points-gcp/) tie the model to the site, and separate check points, kept out of processing, test it. The accuracy and the check method are set in the proposal for the purpose of the job and written into the quality-control report. Resolution (GSD) is the ground area one pixel represents. Accuracy is how close a map position is to the true position. High resolution does not guarantee high accuracy, so the two are defined separately. Processing is done in WebODM (OpenDroneMap). The default coordinate system is the Israeli Grid (EPSG:2039), or another system on request.

## When to use it, and when to choose something else

- **Only a film for an annual report** [industrial photography](/en/industrial-drone-photography/).
- **Earthwork volume along a road** [volume calculation](/en/stockpile-volume-calculation/) with an agreed base surface.
- **Heat or a crack in an envelope** [NDT](/en/drone-ndt-inspection/), not an orthophoto.
- **A statutory plan or a permit map** a background, yes; a signature, no.

## Limits and approvals

The service covers all of Israel, subject to flight approvals and airspace limits at the site. Commercial operation of a small drone requires a small-UAS pilot licence from the Civil Aviation Authority [TO FILL: licence number] and a registered aircraft [TO FILL: registration numbers]. We carry third-party insurance [TO FILL: policy details]. In a built-up area, or when flying over people, vehicles or infrastructure, we need the site owner's written consent and sometimes a special authorisation. We coordinate in advance with the person in charge on site.

Strong wind, rain or fog delays the flight, and we set a nearby alternative date. Capture is planned to stay on the commissioned area, and we do not publish material that identifies people or third-party property. Turnaround depends on area, deliverables and approvals, and is set in the proposal [TO FILL: typical turnaround].

DroneAtlas is not a licensed surveyor. The deliverables are for design, control and quantities. A statutory survey plan, such as a permit map, a registration plan or a boundary determination, is signed by a partner licensed surveyor. Photogrammetry is not used to reconstruct registered boundaries. If a map shows boundaries and is not for registration, the surveyor marks it "not for registration purposes".

## What to prepare

- The site location, a map pin or a boundary file (KML, KMZ, DWG, DXF, SHP or GeoJSON).
- Approximate size, the purpose, the deliverables you want and a preferred date.
- An on-site contact, and access on the flight day.
- If you have them: a georeferenced design file (DWG or SHP) or a previous survey.
- The corridor alignment, the landowners to coordinate with, and any airspace limits you already know.

## FAQ

**Do you fly over a live road?**

Only after an airspace check, written consent where it is required, and sometimes a special authorisation. If it cannot be approved, we say so before promising a date.

**What does the authority receive for planning?**

An orthophoto and a model, on the Israeli Grid, that open in GIS or CAD. Not a signed statutory plan.

**Can a bridge or a solar array be inspected in the same job?**

Corridor mapping and a visual or thermal inspection are two services. If both are needed, both go in the proposal. Thermal inspection is described on its own page, with no camera model and no temperature-accuracy figure.

**Who signs?**

Not DroneAtlas. A partner licensed surveyor, when the document needs a signature.
